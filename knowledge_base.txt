[SECTION: International Travel Policy]
Title: International Travel Policy

Employees who travel internationally for business must obtain approval
from their direct manager before booking any flights.

Economy class is required for flights shorter than six hours.
Business class may be approved for flights longer than six hours.

Employees must purchase company-approved travel insurance before departure.

---

[SECTION: Travel Expense Policy]
Title: Travel Expense Policy

Employees may claim reasonable expenses incurred during approved business travel.

Reimbursable expenses include airfare, hotel accommodation,
local transportation, and meals.

Receipts are required for expenses greater than THB 500.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days after the trip.

---

[SECTION: Domestic Travel Policy]
Title: Domestic Travel Policy

Domestic business travel must be approved by the employee's manager.

Employees should use company-approved transportation when available.

Hotel accommodation may be reimbursed when an overnight stay is required.

---

[SECTION: Remote Work Policy]
Title: Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely for up to two days per week.

Remote work requires approval from the employee's direct manager.

Employees must remain available during normal working hours
and attend required meetings.

---

[SECTION: Annual Leave Policy]
Title: Annual Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 12 days of paid annual leave per year.

Leave requests of more than three consecutive working days
should be submitted at least seven days in advance.

All leave requests require manager approval.

---

[SECTION: Employee Insurance]
Title: Employee Insurance

Full-time employees are covered by the company's group health
and accident insurance plan.

Coverage begins after the employee completes the probation period.

---

[SECTION: International Travel Policy]
Title: Passport and Visa Requirements

Employees are responsible for ensuring that their passports remain valid
for the entire duration of an international business trip.

Required business visas should be arranged before departure.

Reasonable visa application fees may be reimbursed by the company
when the travel has been approved.

---

[SECTION: International Travel Policy]
Title: International Flight Booking

International flights should be booked through the company's approved
travel agency or booking platform.

Employees should make reservations at least 14 days before departure
whenever practical.

Changes to approved bookings should be reported to the employee's manager.

---

[SECTION: International Travel Policy]
Title: International Ground Transportation

Employees may use taxis, public transportation, or company-approved
ride-hailing services while traveling internationally.

Premium transportation services should only be used when necessary
for safety or business requirements.

Receipts should be retained for reimbursement.

---

[SECTION: International Travel Policy]
Title: International Travel Changes

Employees should inform their manager as soon as possible
if an approved international travel itinerary changes.

Additional costs resulting from personal changes to the itinerary
will normally be the employee's responsibility.

Business-related changes may be reimbursed with manager approval.

---

[SECTION: Travel Expense Policy]
Title: Business Travel Meal Expenses

Employees may claim reasonable meal expenses while traveling for business.

Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable unless they are part
of an approved client entertainment expense.

Itemized receipts are required when the total expense exceeds THB 500.

---

[SECTION: Travel Expense Policy]
Title: Foreign Currency Expenses

Expenses paid in foreign currencies should be converted to Thai Baht
when submitting an expense claim.

Employees should use the exchange rate shown on their credit card statement
or another company-approved exchange rate source.

Supporting documentation should be attached to the claim.

---

[SECTION: Travel Expense Policy]
Title: Non-Reimbursable Travel Expenses

Personal entertainment, minibar purchases, personal shopping,
and sightseeing expenses are not reimbursable.

Fines and penalties resulting from an employee's actions
will not normally be reimbursed.

Exceptions require written approval from the employee's manager.

---

[SECTION: Travel Expense Policy]
Title: Corporate Card Usage

Employees issued a corporate card should use it for approved
business travel expenses whenever possible.

Corporate cards must not be used for personal purchases.

Employees are responsible for submitting receipts and supporting documents
for all corporate card transactions.

---

[SECTION: Domestic Travel Policy]
Title: Domestic Flight Booking

Employees traveling domestically by air should select
a reasonably priced economy-class ticket.

Flights should be booked through an approved booking channel.

More expensive flights require justification and manager approval.

---

[SECTION: Domestic Travel Policy]
Title: Domestic Ground Transportation

Employees should use public transportation, company vehicles,
or approved transportation services for domestic business travel.

Rental cars may be used when they are more practical or economical.

Rental car expenses require manager approval.

---

[SECTION: Domestic Travel Policy]
Title: Same-Day Business Travel

Employees are encouraged to complete domestic business trips
within the same day when practical.

An overnight stay may be approved when the travel schedule,
distance, or business requirements make same-day return unreasonable.

---

[SECTION: Domestic Travel Policy]
Title: Domestic Hotel Booking

Hotels for domestic business travel should be selected
from the company's approved hotel list when available.

Employees should choose standard rooms at reasonable business rates.

Room upgrades for personal preference are not reimbursable.

---

[SECTION: Remote Work Policy]
Title: Remote Work Location

Employees may work remotely from their registered residence
or another suitable location approved by their manager.

The remote location must provide reliable internet access
and an appropriate working environment.

Working from another country requires separate approval.

---

[SECTION: Remote Work Policy]
Title: Remote Work Equipment

Employees working remotely may use company-provided laptops,
monitors, and other approved equipment.

Company equipment must be used responsibly and protected
from loss, theft, or unauthorized access.

Employees should report damaged equipment promptly.

---

[SECTION: Remote Work Policy]
Title: Remote Work Data Security

Employees working remotely must follow all company information
security requirements.

Confidential company information should not be accessed
through unsecured public Wi-Fi networks.

A company-approved VPN must be used when required.

---

[SECTION: Remote Work Policy]
Title: Remote Work Attendance

Remote employees are expected to attend scheduled online meetings
and respond to work-related communications during normal working hours.

Employees should update their calendar when they will be unavailable
for an extended period.

---

[SECTION: Annual Leave Policy]
Title: Annual Leave Carryover

Employees are encouraged to use their annual leave within
the year in which it is granted.

Up to five unused annual leave days may be carried forward
to the following year.

Additional carryover requires HR approval.

---

[SECTION: Annual Leave Policy]
Title: Peak Period Leave Requests

Managers may limit annual leave during critical business periods
when sufficient staffing is required.

Employees should submit leave requests as early as possible
during these periods.

Approval will depend on operational requirements.

---

[SECTION: Annual Leave Policy]
Title: Half-Day Annual Leave

Employees may request annual leave in half-day increments.

Half-day leave requests must be submitted through
the normal leave management system.

Manager approval is required before taking the leave.

---

[SECTION: Annual Leave Policy]
Title: Annual Leave Cancellation

Employees who need to cancel approved annual leave
should inform their manager as soon as possible.

Cancelled leave days will be returned to the employee's
available leave balance.

Changes remain subject to manager approval.

---

[SECTION: Employee Insurance]
Title: Health Insurance Claims

Employees should submit eligible health insurance claims
according to the insurance provider's procedures.

Required documents may include medical receipts,
medical certificates, and claim forms.

Employees should contact HR if assistance is required.

---

[SECTION: Employee Insurance]
Title: Dependent Insurance Coverage

Eligible employees may be offered optional health insurance
coverage for qualified dependents.

Employees may be responsible for additional premiums
associated with dependent coverage.

Enrollment conditions are determined by the company's insurance plan.

---

[SECTION: Employee Insurance]
Title: Insurance Coverage During Leave

Employee insurance coverage normally remains active
during approved paid leave.

Coverage during extended unpaid leave may be subject
to different conditions.

Employees should consult HR before beginning extended unpaid leave.

---

[SECTION: Employee Insurance]
Title: Workplace Accident Insurance

Employees who experience a workplace accident should report
the incident to their manager and HR as soon as possible.

Medical treatment related to an eligible workplace accident
may be covered by company insurance.

Required accident reports should be submitted promptly.

---

[SECTION: IT Security Policy]
Title: Password Security

Employees must use strong passwords for company accounts.

Passwords should not be shared with colleagues
or reused across personal and company services.

Employees must change compromised passwords immediately.

---

[SECTION: IT Security Policy]
Title: Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication must be enabled
for company systems that support it.

Employees should never approve an authentication request
that they did not initiate.

Suspicious authentication requests must be reported to IT.

---

[SECTION: IT Security Policy]
Title: Device Encryption

Company laptops and mobile devices containing business data
must use company-approved encryption.

Employees must not disable encryption or other required
security controls.

Security issues should be reported to the IT department.

---

[SECTION: IT Security Policy]
Title: Phishing and Suspicious Emails

Employees should not open suspicious links or attachments
received through email or messaging platforms.

Suspected phishing messages should be reported
to the IT security team.

Employees should not provide passwords or authentication codes
in response to unsolicited requests.

---

[SECTION: Learning and Development Policy]
Title: Employee Training Budget

Eligible full-time employees may receive an annual budget
for job-related training and professional development.

Training expenses require manager approval before registration.

Approved expenses may include course fees and training materials.

---

[SECTION: Learning and Development Policy]
Title: Professional Certification

The company may reimburse fees for professional certifications
relevant to an employee's current role.

Certification requests must be approved before registration.

Proof of payment and completion may be required for reimbursement.

---

[SECTION: Learning and Development Policy]
Title: Mandatory Employee Training

Employees must complete mandatory company training
within the assigned deadline.

Mandatory training may include information security,
workplace safety, and compliance topics.

Managers are responsible for monitoring completion within their teams.

---

[SECTION: Learning and Development Policy]
Title: Study Leave

Employees may request study leave to attend
approved professional training or examinations.

Study leave requires advance approval from the employee's manager.

The number of approved study leave days may depend
on the relevance of the program to the employee's role.

---

[SECTION: Procurement Policy]
Title: Purchase Approval

Business purchases must receive appropriate approval
before an order is placed.

Approval levels depend on the total value of the purchase.

Employees must not divide purchases into smaller transactions
to avoid approval requirements.

---

[SECTION: Procurement Policy]
Title: Vendor Quotations

Employees should obtain multiple vendor quotations
for significant purchases whenever practical.

Price, quality, reliability, and service should be considered
when selecting a supplier.

The lowest-priced supplier is not automatically required.

---

[SECTION: Procurement Policy]
Title: Procurement Conflict of Interest

Employees involved in vendor selection must disclose
any personal or financial relationship with a supplier.

Employees should not participate in procurement decisions
where a conflict of interest exists.

Potential conflicts should be reported to a manager or compliance team.

---

[SECTION: Procurement Policy]
Title: Emergency Purchases

Emergency purchases may be made without the normal
procurement process when immediate action is necessary.

Employees must document the reason for the emergency purchase.

Manager approval should be obtained as soon as practical.

---

[SECTION: Workplace Conduct Policy]
Title: Respectful Workplace

Employees are expected to communicate professionally
and treat colleagues, customers, and business partners with respect.

Threatening, abusive, or intentionally disruptive behavior
is not acceptable in the workplace.

Concerns may be reported to a manager or HR.

---

[SECTION: Workplace Conduct Policy]
Title: Harassment Prevention

Harassment, bullying, and discrimination are prohibited
in all company workplaces and work-related activities.

Employees who experience or observe inappropriate behavior
should report the matter to their manager or HR.

Reports will be handled according to company procedures.

---

[SECTION: Workplace Conduct Policy]
Title: Workplace Dress Standards

Employees should wear clothing appropriate
for their role and working environment.

Departments may establish additional dress requirements
for safety, customer interaction, or professional events.

Employees should consult their manager if they are unsure.

---

[SECTION: Workplace Conduct Policy]
Title: Alcohol and Substance Use

Employees must not work while impaired by alcohol
or illegal substances.

Alcohol may only be consumed at company events
when specifically permitted.

Employees must continue to behave professionally
during company-sponsored events.

---

[SECTION: Company Equipment Policy]
Title: Company Laptop Usage

Company laptops are provided primarily for authorized business purposes.

Employees must install only company-approved software
and follow IT security requirements.

Company laptops should not be shared with unauthorized individuals.

---

[SECTION: Company Equipment Policy]
Title: Company Mobile Devices

Company-issued phones and tablets must be protected
with a password, PIN, or biometric authentication.

Employees should keep devices updated with required
security software.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately.

---

[SECTION: Company Equipment Policy]
Title: Return of Company Equipment

Employees must return company-owned equipment
when requested or when their employment ends.

Returned equipment should include laptops, chargers,
access cards, phones, and other assigned items.

HR or IT will confirm the return of company assets.

---

[SECTION: Company Equipment Policy]
Title: Lost or Damaged Equipment

Employees must promptly report lost, stolen,
or damaged company equipment.

The company will assess whether repair or replacement is required.

Employees may be responsible for costs when damage
results from intentional misuse or serious negligence.
